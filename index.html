Why it's hard

You're not losing on talent. You're losing on the system.

You've applied. You've refreshed. You've rewritten the same résumé at midnight. The effort was never the problem. The hours are.

The refresh race.

8 AM, four tabs, refreshed every minute. By the time you've copied the JD and tailored your résumé, the post is 15 minutes old with 100+ applicants. Alone, you can be fast or you can be good. Rarely both.

Blind volume.

Fifty Easy Applies a day with the same résumé feels like progress. To a recruiter, it's one more generic PDF. Many of those jobs could never have replied: citizens only, clearance required, no sponsorship.

Swipe-and-hope apps.

Apps that turn the job search into swiping. Lots of dopamine, no person checking anything, and you still do most of the work. Activity is not the same as applications that land.

Speed without quality gets ignored. Quality without speed arrives late. We built Jaithraya to do both, every weekday.

How we work

Not more applications. Better-aimed ones.

Most job searches are open loops: send, wait, guess. We run yours like an experiment. Each application is a hypothesis: this person, this job, this résumé. Each one is recorded with evidence, and whatever comes back is a result we review.

  1. 1

    Model

    We turn your history into a structured profile: your roles, your skills, the work you've done.

    baseline
  2. 2

    Select

    Each new posting is checked against that profile. We keep the jobs you can realistically win and the jobs you're authorized for. We drop the rest.

    hypothesis
  3. 3

    Shape

    A résumé tailored for that one job: your summary, skills and bullet points written to the posting.

    variable
  4. 4

    Submit

    A trained person applies, usually within about 12 hours of the nightly job pull, and saves a screenshot as evidence.

    test
  5. 5

    Track

    Interview, assessment, rejection or silence. We record what comes back against the application it answers. Today some of this is done by hand.

    measure
  6. 6

    Improve

    We review what comes back and adjust what we pick and how we write.

    adjust

FAQs

How fast will I get a job?

Rarely in the first month, and we won't pretend otherwise. A hiring decision is the employer's, and anyone who promises you a job is guessing. What we promise is the work: 25–30 well-aimed, tailored applications every weekday, proof of each one, and we track what comes back.

Is this a scam?
  • We never ask for your passport, your I-20 or a placement fee.
  • It's a card subscription, and you can cancel anytime.It's a card subscription. Cancel yourself, anytime.
  • We keep a screenshot of every application, and you can see each one in your dashboard.We keep a screenshot of every application. Ask for any screenshot and we'll send it.
How do I know you really applied?
  • A trained person submits each application and saves a screenshot of the confirmation page.
  • Every application, and its screenshot, is in your dashboard.Ask for any screenshot and we'll send it.
How is this different from auto-apply apps?
  • A person on our team submits every Saaradhi application. It isn't a bot.
  • Each résumé is written for that job, not one PDF sent everywhere.
  • You get a screenshot of each application.
  • Swiping feels productive. We'd rather you see the work.
Why not 100 applications a day?

Blind volume doesn't get replies. We'd rather send 25 that fit than 100 that don't.

Is this safe for my OPT / STEM-OPT?
  • We only match roles you're authorized for.
  • Every work-authorization question is answered truthfully, from the details you give us.
  • One account per site, in your own name. We never create fake or duplicate accounts.
  • We only fill in the details you've given us. We never make up an answer.
What do you change on my résumé?

We tailor your résumé for every job: your summary, skills and bullet points are rewritten for each posting, so the résumé speaks the job's language, and you can see every version we send.

Will you shop my résumé to vendors?

No. We're not a consultancy. Your résumé is never benched, sold or marketed to vendors. It goes only to the jobs we apply to for you.

Do you only show sponsor companies?

No. You see every job you're authorized for. Jobs that say they will not sponsor are removed.

When do you start, and on which days?
  • We start as soon as your profile is set up, usually within a few weekdays.
  • We apply on weekdays, usually within about 12 hours of the nightly job pull.
  • No applications go out on weekends or US federal holidays.
Can I cancel?

Anytime. Message us and it's done before your next billing date. No lock-in on the monthly plan.Anytime, by yourself, from your account.
